- Trade-off: B keeps k nearer the Cajon Pass strong-crust value (Zoback & Healy 1992, k~3 for mu~0.6) and needs MORE overpressure; C weakens the regional field below that constraint but needs the LEAST overpressure. Both keep mu_s constant (no facet-varying friction). Pick by how much regional-stress weakening vs how much fault-zone overpressure is acceptable; the tables above quantify both.
