## Verdict up front

1. **The substep-buffer consumption path on shared fault faces is CORRECT.** With an env-gated
   re-enable of the R-1601-rolled-back gate, np=2 reproduces the np=1 (un-partitioned) trajectory
   **to all printed digits (10 significant), for 12 macro steps**, on every locally constructible
   configuration: pure-shared tri fault, mixed interior+shared ranks (nonzero buffer index base),
   LSW super-critical slip, and rate-and-state aging (the historical law).
2. **Today's R-1601 inline fallback is the partition-dependent path**: it deviates from np=1 from
   step 0 (measured `~1.7e-6` relative on the fixture, growing monotonically).
3. Of the four candidate suspects, **(a), (b) are experimentally ELIMINATED**, (c) is benign, and
   (d) is the *current* path's defect, not a property of the unified path.
4. The historical `τ≈4e28` blow-up **cannot be attributed to buffer consumption itself**. The
   evidence points to the **concurrent R-1600-class per-substep ghost-exchange bugs** (pairwise
   `ExchangeFaceNbrData` mis-gating on ranks without fault shared faces — np=10 symmirror had
   such ranks) corrupting the *inputs* the iterator saw for shared QPs. The R-1601 fallback
   "fixed" the blow-up because the inline path **bypasses the per-substep ghost exchange
   entirely** (it consumes the corrector's own time-integrated exchange) — masking, not fixing,
   an input-path bug that R-1600's fix then addressed. This is PROBABLE, not proven: the
   symmirror mesh is not available locally (gitignored) and np=10 exceeds the fixture.
5. R-1601's own comment attributes the blow-up to the `elem1_on_plus`-vs-`sign_flipped` frame
   mismatch. **That attribution is disproven** (Phase-0 census + these experiments): the two bits
   are *both correct for their respective uses* — `sign_flipped` is the rank-independent frame bit
   (cross-rank `dot(can_n) = +1`), `elem1_on_plus` the rank-local routing bit — and the unified
   path, which uses them exactly as the code does today, is bit-consistent across ranks.

## Symptom

- **Observed (historical):** with `ComputeADERSharedFaceFluxRHS` consuming the iterator's substep
  `I_imp` buffer (the R-1003 §2 gate), `Q` overflowed to `τ≈4e28` within ~7 macro steps at np=10
  on `tpv104_symmirror_1000m.msh` (TPV104, rate-and-state, Brent). R-1601 replaced the gate with
  an unconditional inline one-shot `EvaluateADER*` on shared QPs.
- **Expected:** shared QPs consuming the buffer must reproduce the np=1 answer.
- **Conditions:** np>1 (needs shared fault faces), ADER+substep path.
- **Pre-established:** Phase-0 census falsified the frame-bit hypothesis
  (`sign_flipped` cross-rank dot = **+1.000000000000**; `!elem1_on_plus` dot = **−1.000000000000**).

## Investigation

### E0 — analytic code audit (no execution)

Read both scatter sites end-to-end. Interior (`:4440-4477`) and shared Pass-2 (`:5750-5786`) use
the **identical** (side, sign) convention — plus ⇒ `−F`, minus ⇒ `+F`, same `can_n` for both
sides — and the same `T_can` rotation built from the same frame their respective `I_imp` was
produced in (interior: `!elem1_on_plus`-keyed; shared: `sign_flipped`-keyed; the producer
`EvaluateBulkAtFaultQPsCanonical` uses the same bit per face class: `:2221` vs `:2513`).
Producer/consumer frame consistency holds on both branches. Analytic sign-algebra beyond this was
abandoned as error-prone (too many stacked conventions inside `GodunovFlux::Interior`);
conclusions below are measured, not derived.

### E1 — 2tet, LSW super-critical (τ₀=90 MPa > μ_sσ_n=81.24 MPa), 12 steps, dt=1e-6

| step | np=1 max\|Q\| | np=2 **CONSUME** max\|Q\| | np=2 **fallback** max\|Q\| |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.372493997e+06 | **1.372493997e+06** (=) | 1.372496383e+06 (≠, rel 1.7e-6) |
| 5 | 3.174295587e+06 | **3.174295587e+06** (=) | 3.174309688e+06 (≠) |
| 11 | 5.230482288e+06 | **5.230482288e+06** (=) | 5.230510470e+06 (≠) |

`l2` and `slip2` likewise: CONSUME **identical to all 10 printed digits at every step**; fallback
different at every step. (First run of this experiment used dt=1e-4, above the fixture's CFL
limit — all three configurations grew ×15/step *identically-shaped*; even there CONSUME tracked
np=1 to 10 digits through 12 steps while fallback did not. The dt=1e-6 rerun removes the
instability and the conclusion is unchanged.)

**⇒ (a) ELIMINATED, (b) ELIMINATED** for pure-shared tri faults: a rank-inconsistent `I_imp` or a
wrong scatter slot could not reproduce np=1 to 10 digits through 12 steps of active feedback.

### E2 — tet2x2 (MIXED: rank 0 owns interior AND shared; buffer index base = 3 ≠ 0), LSW

np=2 CONSUME == np=1 in **all five metrics** (max|Q|, l2, slip2, V2, tau2c) to all printed digits,
all 12 steps. np=2 fallback ≠ np=1 at every step.

**⇒ absolute-vs-rebased buffer indexing (R-1304 seam) VERIFIED CORRECT** — a rebased index would
have read interior QPs' `I_imp` for shared QPs and produced immediate garbage.

### E3 — tet2x2, rate-and-state aging (the historical law; a=0.010, b=0.015, Dc=2.0, ψ=0.60)

np=2 CONSUME == np=1 to all printed digits, all 12 steps. (Fallback also matches at this print
precision here — the RS fixture fault is quiet, V≈1e-8 m/s, so the fallback's defect is below 10
digits; the LSW super-critical runs are the discriminating ones for the fallback.)

**⇒ the consumption path is clean for RS/Brent too.**

### E4 — oracle cross-check

With the gate ON, the Phase-0(b) oracle's env-gated RED assertion **turns green**:
`test_shared_fault_substep_parity_np2` at np=2 measures `max|Q_with_buf − Q_no_buf| = 1.15344e+09`
— identical to the interior (np=1) response — i.e. shared QPs now demonstrably consume the buffer.
(Its two characterization assertions correctly invert, as designed.)

### E5 — byte-exactness of the instrumentation when disabled

With both env vars unset: parity oracle np=1/np=2 PASS (np=2 still DISCARDs), census 1/1 + 5/5,
TPV26 baseline smoke `V_max = 0.185543` and forced-rupture smoke `V_max = 0.199649` — the exact
pre-instrumentation values.

### (c) reconcile — assessment

With CONSUME on, both ranks' buffer `I_imp` are bit-identical (E1/E2 imply it; the boss-broadcast
overwrite is then a no-op on `I_imp`), and no divergence appears over 12 steps. The reconcile's
DOFData overwrite ordering is unchanged by the gate. Benign.

## Root cause

- **What (for the observation that started this):** the R-1601 fallback itself. Discarding the
  iterator's `I_imp` on shared QPs and re-solving one-shot over the macro `dt` — with slip already
  advanced to end-of-step by the iterator but the clock at `GetTime()` = step start — makes shared
  fault QPs follow different numerics than interior ones. Measured: np=2 deviates from np=1 from
  step 0; the unified path does not deviate at all.
- **Where:** `dynamic/wave_operator.inl` `ComputeADERSharedFaceFluxRHS`, the unconditional inline
  dispatch + `(void)substep_I_imp_*_flat_` discards (formerly `:5296-5342`).
- **Why (the historical mis-attribution):** the ~7-step `4e28` blow-up that motivated R-1601 was
  attributed to the frame-bit split. The census disproves that mechanism, and no locally
  constructible configuration reproduces any instability through the consumption path. The
  timeline (R-1600 ghost-exchange deadlock/mis-gating bugs found in the SAME debugging session,
  np=10 symmirror having ranks with zero fault faces — the exact R-1600 trigger) plus the fact
  that the fallback *bypasses the per-substep ghost path entirely* make **corrupted per-substep
  ghost inputs (suspect e)** the probable true cause of the historical blow-up. `τ` "fed into
  Brent" is computed from the pointwise ghost states — garbage in, 4e28 out.
- **Impact:** every friction law, every np>1 ADER+substep run; the forced-rupture seam clock lag
  (TPV26/27 review R-002) is a symptom and dissolves when the fallback is retired.

## NEW OPEN FINDING (separate from R-1601): a binary np≥4 trajectory switch

> **RESOLVED 2026-07-10 — NOT a dynamics defect.** Root-caused the same day in

> the "switch" was the station writer's partition-dependent nearest-QP TIE-BREAK at the x2=0
> stations (equidistant between the QP columns at x=∓166.7 m — the two "attractor" values are
> the same field sampled 400 m apart).  Fixed by the deterministic lexicographic tie-break in

> points across its rows; do not read it as a trajectory difference.

## Additional findings (recorded, not chased)

1. **Quad faults abort the production shared-fault reconcile at np>1.** `MakeFaceKey`
   (`dynamic/shared_fault_key.hpp:71-85`) keys on the FIRST THREE face vertices — on a quad the
   two ranks can pick different 3-subsets ⇒ 0 pairs ⇒
   `MFEM_VERIFY(n_paired == fault_qp_buf.size())` aborts (`wave_operator.inl:5816`). Reproduce:
   `SEAS_DIAG_FIXTURE=hex4 mpirun -np 2 ./seas_diag_r1601_experiment`. Latent (production meshes
   are tets), but the struct doc says "triangular fault face" — the limitation is undocumented at
   the abort site.
2. **`SEAS_FS_LIB` probe flakiness:** the per-invocation `std::filesystem` link probe
   (`Makefile:567-574`) failed transiently once, injecting `-lstdc++fs` into a macOS link
   (`ld: library not found`). Workaround: `make ... SEAS_FS_LIB=""`. Retry also clears it.
